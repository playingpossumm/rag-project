Parameter study — camera elevation

The projection used to be one isometric angle driving horizontal spread and camera height together, which is why turning it up for depth also tipped the scene over and looked down on it. They are separate now: SPREAD 0.97 fans the ground axes out, and RISE is how far above the ground plane the camera sits. Zero is eye level, a true isometric is 0.5, the old 60° was 0.87. Shipped at 0.15.
