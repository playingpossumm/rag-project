Camera elevation sweep

The projection used to be one isometric angle, which drove the horizontal spread and the camera height together — so turning it up for more depth also tipped the scene over and looked down on it from above. They are two numbers now. SPREAD fans the ground axes out horizontally and stays at 0.97; RISE is how far above the ground plane the camera sits, and it is the one that reads as viewing angle. Zero is eye level. A true isometric is 0.5.

What was shipped — the old 60° isometric, RISE 0.87

Looking down on the drawing from most of the way above it. The panels turn far enough that the matrices read as slivers and the index field collapses into a solid mass — which was most of what made it look like a stack of layers.

Renders are the live page at each value, nothing retouched. Height still projects 1:1 to screen, so lowering RISE flattens the ground plane without collapsing the vertical gap between dense retrieval and BM25. Set it with RISE in ui/pipeline-map.js; shipped now at 0.15.
