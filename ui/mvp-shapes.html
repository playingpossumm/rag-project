Stage shapes

The isometric plate stays. The question is only what goes inside it, and whether two stages that do opposite things should look different. Five stages are drawn in each option, at rest, with a real run's counts.

A. As it is now

one treatment

Every stage is the same grid of cells. The picture says seven steps happened and nothing else: dense retrieval and BM25 are drawn identically although one matches meaning and the other matches exact words.

B. Same grid, different fill

low risk

The plate and the cell grid are unchanged, so the drawing keeps its rhythm. What differs is the marking: dense scatters its cells, BM25 fills whole rows because it matches whole words, fusion interleaves two sources, the cross-encoder shows the reorder, and the cap draws a bar over the rows it refuses. Cheapest to build and the smallest departure.

C. Different structure per stage

most distinct

The plate stays isometric, but the grid itself changes shape: the index is a deep stack of sheets, dense is a loose field, BM25 is a column of bars, fusion is two stacks merged into one, the cap is a slotted grate with its blocked rows visible. Each stage is recognisable at a glance without its label. Biggest change and the most work.
